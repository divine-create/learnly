# CodeBridge Nigeria — Marketing & Sales Plan (2026)

---

## 1. Executive Summary

CodeBridge Nigeria is a B2B2C SaaS product: schools pay the subscription, but five distinct stakeholder groups (admins, teachers, students, parents, super-admin) all derive value. The sales motion is therefore **top-down** (sell the school principal/proprietor → they activate teachers → teachers activate students → parents follow). The marketing motion is **bottom-up awareness** (students and parents create demand that pushes principals to buy).

**Revenue target — Year 1:** 120 schools × average ₦250k/term × 3 terms = **₦90M ARR**
**Revenue target — Year 2:** 500 schools × average ₦300k/term × 3 terms = **₦450M ARR**

## 2. Market Analysis

### 2.1 Total Addressable Market (TAM)

| Segment | Numbers |
|---|---|
| Nigerian K-12 students | ~65 million |
| Registered private schools | ~70,000 (NUC/UBEC data) |
| Private schools with computer labs | ~18,000 (est.) |
| Private schools that pay for edtech tools | ~4,500 (est.) |
| Near-term serviceable market (SSA) | ~1,200 schools |

Private schools are the primary beachhead. They have fee-paying parents, existing ICT infrastructure, and proprietors with purchasing authority. Government/public schools are a Phase 2 play via state ministry bulk deals.

### 2.2 Competitive Landscape

| Competitor | Weakness you exploit |
|---|---|
| **Kolibri / Khan Academy** | Not Nigeria-specific, no AI tutor, no coding focus |
| **Ulearn / Passnownow** | Exam prep only (WAEC/JAMB), no project-based coding |
| **Local ICT teachers** | No gamification, no parent visibility, no AI grading |
| **Scratch/Code.org** | No curriculum alignment, no teacher management layer |
| **WhatsApp groups** | Zero structured learning, no accountability |

**CodeBridge's moat:** NERDC-aligned, Naira-priced, offline-capable PWA, Cody AI tutor with Nigerian analogies, parent weekly reports, school admin dashboard — all in one.

### 2.3 Nigerian School Calendar

| Term | Duration | Sales implication |
|---|---|---|
| First Term | Sept – Dec | **Primary purchase window**: budget set, new session starts |
| Second Term | Jan – Mar | Renewal + upsell window |
| Third Term | Apr – Jun | Demo season, pilot schools, next-year commitment |
| Long Vac | July – Aug | Conference season, bulk deals, ministry pitches |

All sales efforts should peak **6–8 weeks before term start** (mid-July for First Term, mid-November for Second Term).

## 3. Customer Segments & Personas

### Segment A — Proprietors / Principals of Private Schools (Economic Buyer)
**Profile:** Owns or manages a private school, 150–800 students, Lagos/Abuja/PH/Enugu. Focused on competitive differentiation, WAEC results, and parent retention.

**Pain:** "I need to show parents we are modern. Other schools are advertising tech. I don't know where to start."

**Message:** "Be the first school in your estate to offer AI-powered coding classes. Give parents a weekly progress report every Sunday morning — automated."

**Where to reach:** WhatsApp groups for school owners, NAPPS (National Association of Proprietors of Private Schools) meetings, Lagos State Private Schools Association, LinkedIn.

---

### Segment B — School ICT Teachers (Champion / Power User)
**Profile:** The ICT teacher who currently teaches Microsoft Word and Excel to JSS 2. Overworked, limited resources, no coding background in many cases.

**Pain:** "I have 40 students and no way to know who understands and who is lost. I spend hours marking assignments."

**Message:** "Upload your lesson notes once. Cody teaches, quizzes, and marks for you. You focus on the students who are genuinely stuck."

**Where to reach:** TRCN (Teachers Registration Council of Nigeria) events, ICT teacher WhatsApp groups, Facebook groups ("Nigerian Teachers Hub"), LinkedIn.

---

### Segment C — Parents (Influencer / Advocate)
**Profile:** Middle-class parent, pays ₦80k–₦500k/term school fees, concerned about child's future employability, follows tech trends.

**Pain:** "I pay so much in school fees but I don't know what my child is actually learning day to day."

**Message:** "Every Sunday morning, receive your child's coding progress report. See their XP, streaks, quiz scores, and badges — without opening an app."

**Where to reach:** Instagram, WhatsApp broadcast lists via the school, parenting Facebook groups ("Naija Mums", "Nigerian Parents"), LinkedIn for working parents.

---

### Segment D — State Ministries of Education (Enterprise)
**Profile:** State officials responsible for curriculum implementation, looking to show tech adoption stats.

**Pain:** "We need to demonstrate digital literacy outcomes for our schools under the National Digital Economy policy."

**Message:** "License CodeBridge for all public secondary schools in your state. Get a real-time state-wide dashboard of coding adoption, lesson completion, and student performance."

**Where to reach:** Direct government relations, EdTech Nigeria conferences, NIESV/NITDA channels.

## 4. Pricing Strategy

### Current Tiers

| Plan | Price/Term | Students | Teachers | Best For |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Starter** | ₦150,000 | Up to 100 | Up to 5 | Small private primaries |
| **Growth** | ₦300,000 | Up to 300 | Up to 15 | Mid-size private schools |
| **Institution** | ₦600,000 | Unlimited | Unlimited | Large schools, groups of schools |

### Pricing Anchors
- Starter = ₦1,500/student/term at 100 students. Equivalent to 1 hour of private coding tutoring.
- Growth = ₦1,000/student/term. Cheaper than a textbook per subject.
- Institution = present as "unlimited growth, fixed cost" — powerful for proprietors planning to expand.

### Recommended Add-ons (Phase 2 revenue levers)

| Add-on | Price | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Parent SMS alerts | ₦10,000/term | Weekly SMS + badge notifications |
| Ministry State Dashboard | ₦5M–₦15M/year | Custom SLA, bulk schools |
| Teacher CPD Certificate | ₦5,000/teacher | Accredited by TRCN (pursue partnership) |
| School Certificate Branding | ₦20,000/term | Custom school logo on student certificates |

### Discounting Rules
- **No discount below 10% without manager approval** — protect Naira pricing.
- **Multi-term discount:** Pay 2 terms upfront, get 10% off. Pay annual (3 terms), get 20% off.
- **Referral:** Referring school gets ₦30,000 credit. Referred school gets first month free during trial.
- **NAPPS member discount:** 5% — negotiated via association partnership.

## 5. Go-to-Market Strategy

### Phase 1 — Beachhead (Months 1–3): Lagos Private Schools

**Why Lagos:** Highest concentration of tech-forward private schools, highest parent income, most active NAPPS chapter, easiest logistics for in-person demos.

**Target:** 20 pilot schools in Lagos Island, Lekki, Victoria Island, Ikeja, Surulere.

**Tactic:**
1. Identify ICT teacher in each target school via LinkedIn/WhatsApp groups.
2. Offer ICT teacher a **free 30-day pilot** — no credit card, instant school registration.
3. ICT teacher runs one class with Cody during the pilot.
4. After 2 weeks, send teacher a "pilot results email" showing class performance data.
5. Teacher takes the data to principal → principal sees the upsell story → closes.

**Goal:** 20 pilots → 12 paid conversions at ₦150k–₦300k average = ₦2.4M MRR from Lagos alone.

---

### Phase 2 — Expand (Months 4–8): Abuja + Port Harcourt + Enugu

Replicate the Lagos playbook in 3 more cities. Hire 1 city-based Sales Development Representative (SDR) per city.

**Channel additions:**
- Sponsor ICT teacher workshop in each city (₦200k/workshop sponsorship — logo, 20-min pitch slot).
- Partner with school uniform/textbook suppliers who visit schools regularly → referral arrangement.

---

### Phase 3 — Scale (Months 9–12): Government & Ministry Deals

1. Present aggregated anonymized data from Phase 1–2 pilots to Lagos State Ministry of Education.
2. Apply to NITDA EdTech incubator / NCC Digital Economy fund for visibility.
3. Pitch to 5 state governors' offices with digital economy agendas (Anambra, Rivers, Enugu, FCT, Lagos).

## 6. Sales Process

### 6.1 Pipeline Stages

```
LEAD → QUALIFIED → DEMO BOOKED → PILOT ACTIVE → PROPOSAL SENT → CLOSED WON
```

| Stage | Definition | Target Conv. Rate | Responsible |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lead | School identified, contact found | — | SDR |
| Qualified | Confirmed: ICT teacher + 50+ students + pays for tools | 40% | SDR |
| Demo booked | Live demo scheduled (Zoom or on-site) | 60% | AE |
| Pilot active | School registered, first class run | 70% | AE + CS |
| Proposal sent | Pricing email + invoice | 80% | AE |
| Closed Won | Payment received | 65% | AE |

**Overall funnel:** 100 leads → 40 qualified → 24 demos → 17 pilots → 14 proposals → **9 paid customers**

### 6.2 The Demo Script (30 minutes)

1. **0–5 min:** "Tell me about your current ICT programme" — discovery, not pitch.
2. **5–10 min:** Log in as Teacher → upload a PDF lesson note → watch Cody auto-generate quiz. ("This just saved you 2 hours of marking.")
3. **10–15 min:** Switch to Student view → show Cody chatting with a student using Suya/Jollof analogies → student earns XP and badge.
4. **15–20 min:** Switch to Parent view → show weekly report email preview. ("Parents forward this to grandparents on WhatsApp — free PR for your school.")
5. **20–25 min:** School Admin → show leaderboard, attendance, bulk CSV import. ("You onboard 200 students in 10 minutes.")
6. **25–30 min:** Pilot offer + pricing. Always close with: "Which plan fits your student count?"

### 6.3 Objection Handling

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| "We don't have reliable internet" | "CodeBridge works offline. Lessons are cached as a PWA — students load once on Wi-Fi, then work without data." |
| "Our students don't have laptops" | "It runs on any Android phone or tablet. A ₦40k Tecno can run the full platform." |
| "The price is too high" | "₦150k/term for 100 students is ₦1,500 per student — less than one private lesson. You're replacing 5 hours of the ICT teacher's marking per week." |
| "Let me think about it" | "Absolutely. Let me set up a free 30-day pilot — zero risk. I'll check back in 2 weeks with your class performance data." |
| "Our proprietor won't approve it" | "Would it help if I joined your next meeting with the proprietor? I can show the parent reporting feature — they love seeing Sunday morning progress reports." |
| "We use [X competitor]" | "Does [X] generate Cody-style quizzes from your own teacher notes, or is it generic content? Let me show you the difference." |

## 8. Customer Success & Retention

Acquisition is expensive; retention is where the unit economics work.

### 8.1 Onboarding Sequence (First 30 Days)

| Day | Action | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | School registered → Admin receives welcome email with setup checklist | Automated |
| 1 | CS calls admin: "Walk me through your class structure — I'll set up the first class for you" | CS rep |
| 3 | Bulk CSV import of students completed together | CS rep |
| 7 | First lesson published by teacher; CS sends "Your first lesson is live" congratulations | Automated |
| 14 | CS sends admin "2-week health check": # students active, avg quiz score, % lessons completed | Automated dashboard email |
| 21 | CS calls teacher: "What's one thing Cody could do better for your class?" | CS rep |
| 30 | Admin receives "Month 1 Impact Report" (lessons, XP earned, quiz scores, badges) | Automated PDF |

### 8.2 Health Score

Track a simple health score per school (0–100):

```
Health = (% students active this week × 40)
       + (# lessons published this term / target × 30)
       + (parent report emails opened / sent × 30)
```

Schools below 50: CS calls within 48 hours. Schools above 80: ask for testimonial/referral.

### 8.3 Renewal Motion

- **45 days before term end:** Admin receives "Renewal Reminder" with impact stats for the term.
- **30 days before:** AE calls to discuss upgrade (Starter → Growth).
- **15 days before:** Offer multi-term discount if paying for next full session.
- **Day of expiry:** Platform downgrades gracefully (read-only mode, no new students). Never hard-cut access immediately — gives goodwill buffer.

### 8.4 Upsell Triggers

| Signal | Action |
|---|---|
| Student count approaching plan limit | "You're at 87/100 students — upgrade to Growth before the new term" |
| Teacher count hitting limit | "3 teachers waiting to join — Growth plan unlocks 15" |
| High parent engagement | "Your parents open the weekly report at 78% rate — add SMS alerts to reach the other 22%" |
| School owns multiple campuses | "Institution plan covers all your campuses under one subscription" |

## 15. The Single Biggest Lever

Of everything above, the **ICT teacher pilot-to-paid motion** is the highest-leverage, lowest-cost path to early revenue. An ICT teacher who runs one successful Cody session will advocate harder than any salesperson. The product does the selling — the job of marketing is only to get the teacher into that first session.

Every other channel either feeds that moment (ads, content, SDR outreach) or scales it (referrals, events, ministry deals). Start there.
